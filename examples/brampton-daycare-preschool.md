# Ontario Daycare and Child Care Centre Permit Checklist Generator - Ontario

**City:** Brampton | **Area:** 2200 sq ft | **Licensed child capacity:** 32

> Preliminary checklist generated with the free open-source Ontario Daycare and Child Care Centre Permit Checklist Generator by Dust 2 Engineering Inc., Mississauga, Ontario. Verify every item with your designer and the local building department before submitting.

## 1. Application basics (always required)
- [ ] Municipal building permit application form, completed and signed by the owner or authorized agent
- [ ] Schedule 1: Designer Information form for each qualified designer on the project
- [ ] Owner authorization if the applicant is not the property owner, plus the lease for a tenant fit-out if the city asks for it

## 2. Architectural drawings
- [ ] Floor plan with a room for each age group, the licensed capacity of each room, washrooms, sleep areas and the secure entry
- [ ] Zoning review first: child care centres are not permitted in every zone - confirm the use at this address before design starts
- [ ] Barrier-free entrance, path of travel and barrier-free washroom where required
- [ ] Fire separations from neighbouring units, exit signs and emergency lighting shown on plan
- [ ] Child-height fixtures and finishes that can be washed and disinfected in play and washroom areas
- [ ] Sign permit is separate from the building permit - apply for it separately

## 3. Ventilation and HVAC
- [ ] HVAC drawings: heating, cooling and ventilation for play rooms, sleep rooms and offices
- [ ] Washroom exhaust shown with equipment schedules
- [ ] Kitchen exhaust added if food is cooked on site (see the kitchen section)

## 4. Plumbing and electrical
- [ ] Washroom fixtures counted per child from the Ontario Building Code child care table: 1 water closet and lavatory per 10 children under 2, per 10 children aged 2 to 5, and per 15 children of each sex aged 6 to 9
- [ ] Hot water tempered to prevent scalding at fixtures children use
- [ ] Laundry sink and mop sink, backflow preventer where required
- [ ] Electrical drawings: panels, lighting, receptacles with covers in child areas, emergency lighting

## Kitchen and food preparation
- [ ] Food premises layout: preparation area separated from play areas, handwash lavatory inside the preparation area
- [ ] Three-compartment sink or commercial dishwasher, plus food storage shown on plan
- [ ] Grease interceptor if cooking produces grease-laden waste
- [ ] Public health food premises approval is separate from the building permit - notify public health before opening

## Outdoor play area
- [ ] Fenced outdoor play area shown on the site plan, with self-closing gates and no direct exit to a parking lot or road
- [ ] Play space sized per child under the provincial child care licensing rules
- [ ] Play equipment is expected to meet CSA Z614 - keep the equipment certificates for the licensing visit

## 5. Other approvals (not part of the building permit)
- [ ] Provincial child care licence under the Child Care and Early Years Act (Ministry of Education) - separate from, and usually slower than, the building permit
- [ ] Public health inspection of the centre before opening
- [ ] Fire safety plan and fire department approval for the occupancy
- [ ] Business licence from the city, after the permit is closed

## Before you submit
- [ ] One combined PDF set: architectural, mechanical, plumbing, electrical and structural (if any), all consistent with each other
- [ ] All drawing sets use the same address, unit number, floor area and counts
- [ ] Designs that require an engineer must be stamped by a licensed Ontario P.Eng.; BCIN designer information where applicable
- [ ] Book a pre-consultation or zoning check with the city if the use or layout is not clearly permitted
